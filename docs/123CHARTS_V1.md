# 123Charts V1 — local product authority

The user-approved local plan replaces the earlier 88CN product positioning and implementation-agent role restrictions. The repository's existing Git history and adjacent projects are preserved.

## Product contract

- Free global project discovery and website comparison, initially AI coding, workflow automation and knowledge search/RAG. English default, all active surfaces bilingual.
- Public research profiles, verified management identity and approved contribution enrollment are independent. A profile may rank without enrolling; an enrolled project may have no traffic.
- Ten distinct relevant peer websites and valid evidence for five to ten distinct peers. Multiple images of one peer count once. Different paths, www/subdomain aliases and repositories cannot fill duplicate slots.
- Source-published month calendar; initially the three latest accepted months. Historic records are kept separately. No automatic current-date assumption.
- Private original images, raw OCR, extracted candidates, explicit review channel/reference and evidence state. OCR never proves authenticity. No fixed tolerance or confidence-weighted visits.
- One category/source/month/metric/scope per chart. Versioned traffic, immutable snapshots, visible correction explanations. Missing data is unknown. Same-source duplicates are not independent measurements.
- Four free daily recommendation slots: two new enrollments, one adopted contribution, one low-exposure project. Known controlling entities are deduplicated. Recommendation eligibility cannot change data rank.
- Approved applicants receive a profile, official link and private ten-peer observation group. Voluntary, revocable sharing and labeled PNGs. Project management requires a separate approved identity claim.
- Later information updates preserve the observation group and are reviewed incrementally. Free factual correction does not require enrollment contribution.
- Actual visibility and outbound events, filtered tests/bots/self events, daily visitor/project/UTC-date click estimates and attributed surfaces. No signup/customer/load guarantee.
- Saved projects, category/project follows, opt-in email and in-app summaries. Local SMTP is captured in Mailpit.
- Newly adopted peer profiles remain unpublished until factual bilingual content and a reference are reviewed.

## Local-only boundaries

Next.js 16, React 19, local Supabase PostgreSQL/Auth/private Storage/Mailpit, one bounded asynchronous worker and replaceable OCR. Current local URLs use 127.0.0.1:3100; formal brand/domain is 123Charts / 123charts.com. Existing historical endpoint writes are retired in the local preview.

Default preview data is explicitly synthetic for visits, rankings and participation examples. Real research profiles and demonstration traffic are not conflated. All local HTML is noindex; drafts, private records and raw screenshot material are excluded from public projections.

No GitHub writes, push, PR, DNS, production database or server deployment is part of this delivery. TabAPI, paid vision models, GSC/GA4 integration, growth charts, a points marketplace and broad automated comparison pages remain deferred. Real acquisition cost, sustainable operating cost and actual user growth require a later real-data pilot and cannot be established by demo fixtures.
